The Challenge

What We Were Dealing With

Manual processes that slowed down delivery operations and created accountability gaps.

📋

No Centralized Tracking

ASN records were spread across spreadsheets. No single source of truth for delivery status.

⏱️

Manual SLA Monitoring

Lead times and delivery deadlines were tracked manually — easy to miss, hard to audit.

👤

No Personnel Accountability

No clear record of who handled which delivery, when it was released, or what the outcome was.

📞

Customer Visibility Gap

Customers had no way to check their delivery status — everything went through a phone call.

Our Solution

A Purpose-Built
Delivery System

The GMPI ASN Portal is a custom web application built specifically for our operations — not a generic tool, but one shaped around how GMPI actually works.

Data Security

Built With Security in Mind

🔐

SHA-256 Password Hashing

All passwords and PINs are hashed with a salt before storage. Plain-text passwords are never saved in the database.

👤

Role-Based Access Control

Each user role sees only what they need. Dispatchers cannot access customer data. Personnel cannot access settings.

🔄

Forced Password Change

New accounts are issued a default password and required to change it on first login before accessing any feature.

☁️

Firebase Firestore

Data is stored on Google's Firebase — enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure with automatic backups and uptime SLAs.

🚫

Account Deactivation

Superadmin can instantly deactivate any account. Deactivated users are blocked from login immediately.

✏️

Superadmin Controls

Full user management: add, edit, reset password, activate/deactivate — all from within the portal itself.
