1. Purpose

What this system is for

The GMPI ASN Portal is a web-based delivery management system used to create, track, and confirm Advance Shipping Notices (ASNs) for outbound deliveries. It connects three key parties — the Dispatch team, the Delivery personnel, and the Customer — in a single real-time platform accessible from any device with a browser.

4. Dispatch Procedure

📋 Dispatch  Dispatch In-Charge

Login: Select "Dispatch" on the landing page and enter your dispatch PIN.

Step-by-Step

1

Upload SI Invoice Report (Staging)

Go to the Upload tab. Under SI Invoice Staging, upload the full SI Invoice Report from your ERP (Excel format). The system deduplicates automatically — you can re-upload at any time to keep staging up to date.

This is a standing upload. You do not need to clear old data first.

2

Upload the Delivery Manifest

Still on the Upload tab, under Manifest Upload, upload the Delivery Planning Manifest (Excel). Select the correct route sheet from the dropdown that appears.

3

Parse & Generate ASNs

Click Parse & Generate ASNs →. The system matches the manifest against the SI staging data and creates one ASN per customer per route. A summary will appear showing how many ASNs were generated.

Important: Ensure the SI Invoice Report has been uploaded first. ASNs will not generate correctly if the staging data is empty or outdated.
4

Review Generated ASNs

Go to the ASNs tab to view all generated ASNs. Filter by status or search by customer name. Each ASN shows the assigned delivery personnel, route, and expected delivery date.

5

Monitor via Live Tracker

The Tracker tab shows the live status of all delivery runs in real time. It auto-refreshes every 60 seconds. Each run shows stop-level progress — which customers have been tagged and which are still pending.

6

Generate Run Reports

Go to the Reports tab. Select a Control Number and Released Date, then click Generate Report to produce a full delivery run summary. Reports can be exported for record-keeping.

5. Delivery Procedure

🚚 Delivery  Delivery Personnel / Driver

Login: Select "Delivery" on the landing page and enter your Personnel Code (e.g. DP-001).

Step-by-Step

1

Select Your Run

After entering your code, a selection screen will show your assigned Control Numbers and dates. Select the correct run for today's delivery.

2

Start the Delivery Run

On the run screen, tap Start Delivery Run. This sets all your assigned ASNs to In Transit simultaneously — signaling to dispatch that the run has begun.

Note: This action cannot be undone. Only tap Start when the truck has physically left the warehouse.
3

View Your Stops

Your delivery stops are listed in order by Stop Number. Each stop shows the customer name, number of invoices, and current tagging status. Tap a stop to expand its Sales Invoices.

4

Tag Each Delivery

Tap Tap to tag → on an SI to open the tagging screen. Review the customer details, items, and delivery instructions, then select the delivery outcome:

✅ Delivered — items successfully handed over
🔴 Returned — customer refused or unavailable
🔄 Rescheduled — moved to a future delivery date

A reason is required when tagging as Returned or Rescheduled.

5

Enter Recipient Name & Take Photo

Enter the name of the person who received the delivery. Tap Take / Choose Photo to capture or upload a Proof of Delivery (POD) photo.

6

Submit the Delivery Tag

Tap Submit Delivery Tag. The tag is saved and the SI is marked as done. Repeat for each SI at the stop. Once all SIs at a stop are tagged, the stop collapses automatically.

📦

6. Customer Procedure

📦 Customer  Consignee / Customer

Login: Select "Customer" on the landing page and enter your Customer Code.
1

Enter Customer Code & Select Run

Enter your customer code, then select the Control Number and date for the delivery you want to track.

2

View Incoming Orders

Your ASN card will appear, showing all Sales Invoices under your delivery. Each SI lists the invoice number, items, quantities, and current delivery status.

The customer view is read-only. No tagging or editing is available from this screen.
